Robotics open-problems matrix

Window 2025-01 to 2026-09 · compiled 2026-09-16 · 15 open, 5 partial, 0 solved. Status is the field's, not this page's.

Learning-based control and VLAs
The field's consensus milestones remain collectively unmet: multimodality, reasoning, data, evaluation, cross-robot action generalization, efficiency, whole-body coordination, safety, agents, and coordination with humans.
These are stated as the ten open challenges, i.e. milestones the field agrees are ahead, not a checklist any single system meets.
openconsensus"10 Open Challenges" position paper (2026)
Semantic-vs-embodied gap: VLA systems often understand the instruction but fail to execute the task, because they inherit LLM/VLM priors with no physical-interaction experience.
A robustness/grounding problem, not a language problem; more language pretraining does not close it.
openreportedVLA semantic-vs-embodied gap paper (2026)
Capability/robustness trade-off: an information-theoretic bound argues you cannot get both for free.
If the bound holds, it reframes many "make it more capable" results as silently trading away robustness.
openreportedCapability/robustness trade-off (information-theoretic bound) (2026)
Discrete action tokenization itself limits VLA scaling.
A design-choice critique; continuous / diffusion action heads are the contender but not a settled replacement.
openreportedDiscrete action tokenization limits VLA scaling (2026)
Skill portability: commercial robot-skill marketplaces ship one-tap skills only as static playback. Open sub-problems are adaptation, cross-embodiment portability, provenance, safety verification, composition, and standardisation.
Addressed directly by the sibling repo skill-package-spec, which ships a format for provenance + adaptation + safety manifest + composition.

Evaluation and benchmarks
There is no SWE-bench for robotics: every paper uses a slightly different task suite, camera setup, and embodiment. The stated need is a small sharp benchmark plus a large contamination-controlled real-world one.
The infrastructure half (contamination control, embodiment tagging, reproducible scorecards) is addressed by the sibling repo robobench-harness; the community benchmark itself remains open.
openconsensusRobotics evaluation consensus (surveys/position papers) (2026)
Deformable-object manipulation lacks a universal high-DoF dexterous hand with tactile sensors and comprehensive evaluation at scale.openconsensusDeformable-object manipulation survey (2026)
World models and simulation
Learned simulators are now used as RL environments and even co-evolve with policies, but fidelity, long-horizon consistency, and trustworthy use as sim-to-real bridges remain open.
Usable today; the open part is knowing WHEN to trust a rollout. The sibling repo world-model-eval measures long-horizon drift and rollout trust.
partialconsensusWorld-model survey (2026)
Physics-sim limits: humanoid control tasks particularly highlight the simulation-to-reality gap, suggesting improved physics modeling is needed.
Contact-rich and humanoid dynamics are where the gap is widest.
partialreportedMuJoCo Playground writeup (2026)
Safety
Adversarial and backdoor attacks and environmental jailbreaks (malicious text/objects in the scene that steer the robot) have no robust defenses or evaluation standards yet.
The evaluation-standard half is addressed by the sibling repo vla-scene-redteam (threat taxonomy, defenses, attack-success-rate scoring); robust guaranteed defenses remain open.
